For You, Sachi ❤️

Happy Birthday meri Sachi ji, ❤️😂

Honestly, mujhe nahi pata ye sab tujhe kaise explain karu. Mujhe pata hai ki humara aage shayad koi future nahi hai, aur shayad isi wajah se meri feelings aur bhi confusing hain. But jo feeling mujhe tere liye hai, wo maine honestly kisi aur ke liye kabhi feel nahi ki.

I know mere actions ki wajah se maine tujhe hurt kiya hai, aur jo bhi hua— flirting, casual dates, FWB ya meri stupid harkatein—shayad wo sab mere liye casual tha, but tere case mein kabhi bhi waisa nahi raha.

Tu genuinely mera type hai, aur honestly ab lagta hai ki mere type ka koi aur ho bhi nahi sakta. Tu mujhse choti hai, but maturity mein mujhse bohot aage hai. Main abhi bhi childish harkatein karta rehta hu, cheezein kharab kar deta hu, kabhi bina soche kuch kar deta hu, but teri wahi maturity, teri baatein aur jis tarah tu cheezein samajhti hai, mujhe sabse zyada pasand hai.

Aur sundar toh tu hai hi… bilkul apsara jaisi ❤️. Mujhe pata hai ki humara koi future nahi hai, phir bhi pata nahi kyun main tujhe move on karke bhula nahi paata. Shayad kyunki tu mere liye sirf ek person nahi hai.

Tu mere liye woh hai jiske liye main chahe kitni bhi bakchodi kar lu, kitni bhi galtiyan kar lu, phir bhi dil se hamesha care karta rahunga.

Main perfect nahi hu, aur ye toh tu mujhse better jaanti hai. Main harkatein kar deta hu, kabhi khud hi sab kharab kar deta hu, but one thing I know—I'll always be there for you, jis bhi way mein tu mujhe apni life mein rakhna chahe.

Aur haan, main ladki-baaz hu 😂, but tu… tu alag hai. Bohot alag. Tere liye jo feel karta hu, wo kisi aur ke liye kabhi nahi aaya.

So once again,

Happy Happy Sachi Divas 😋😌💋😂💦🫂🥳🎉💃🕺🎀🤸🤪🤟🙃💗, Sachi ji… ❤️

Hamesha khush reh, aise hi sundar reh, aur haan, mujhe tolerate karne ke liye tujhe ek lifetime achievement award toh definitely milna chahiye. 😂🏆
